# Music v2 (-m2): a different score for each scenario, built Oct 27, 2026
All tracks are original. They were synthesized on the box by `../music/build/mk2.py` (numpy: Karplus-Strong plucks, additive piano, FM bells and electric piano, filtered-saw brass, pads, synth drums, and an FFT reverb).
No samples or third-party audio are used. They're our own work, released as **CC0**, so they're free for commercial use.
Each track is synced to its video's scene changes: the tempo is adjusted per section so each cut lands on a downbeat with a hit. A fill or swell plays every 4 bars when there's no cut.
The music ducks about 15 dB under the VO, is louder in gaps, and fades out over the last 2 s. Loudness is about -13.7 LUFS, true peak at or below -0.5 dBTP.
The v2 versions use the clean VO stem. The v1 versions are layered over the existing mix, so the old quiet calm bed is still underneath.
